# Adding a review to your website

Your home page shows what clients have said about working with you. The reviews
live in the same spreadsheet as your retreats, on a second tab called
**Reviews**. Add a row, click Publish, and the page updates about a minute
later.

If you have already added a retreat, this works exactly the same way, and it is
the same Publish button.

## The Reviews tab

Each review is one row.

| Column | What to put in it |
|---|---|
| Name | How the person should be credited, for example Sarah M. Whatever they are happy to be called publicly |
| Review | Their words. Up to 400 characters, which is about four or five lines |
| Stars | Pick a number from 1 to 5 from the list in the cell |

Leave their words as they wrote them. If someone reviewed you in Spanish, put
the Spanish in, and it stays Spanish on the website in every language. Their
review is theirs, so nothing is translated on your behalf.

## Publishing

1. Add or change your rows.
2. In the menu bar at the top, click **Munay**, then **Publish to website**.
3. If something is wrong, a message tells you which row and what to fix.
   Nothing is published until it is right.
4. When it says Published, wait about a minute, then refresh your website.

The Publish button checks the Retreats tab and the Reviews tab together, so one
click covers both.

## Things worth knowing

**Order is up to you.** Reviews appear in the order the rows are in. To put a
favourite first, drag its row to the top.

**Up to six show at once, three at a time.** Visitors move through them with the
arrows underneath, and they rotate on their own. On a phone they see one at a
time and can swipe.

**You can list up to twelve.** If you add more, the first twelve are used.

**Reviews never expire.** Unlike retreats, a review stays until you delete it.

**Having none is fine.** With the Reviews tab empty, the home page shows a short
quote in that spot instead, so it never looks unfinished. That is what is there
now, before you add your first one.

**Deleting a row removes that review** from the website at your next publish.

## If something goes wrong

**The message says the review is too long.** It tells you the character count
and how many to cut. Long reviews make one card much taller than the others, so
pick the strongest few sentences. If you want the whole thing on the site, ask
Ash.

**The message mentions the stars.** Click the cell and pick a number from the
list rather than typing one.

**The message mentions the heading row.** Something has happened to the top row
of the Reviews tab, or the tab has been renamed. Get in touch rather than
guessing.

**You published and nothing changed.** Wait two minutes and refresh once more.
Your browser sometimes holds on to the old version. If it still has not changed,
get in touch.

## A note on asking for reviews

Ask people to keep it to a few sentences and to say what changed for them,
rather than only that it was good. Those read best on the card, and they are the
ones that help someone decide to book.
